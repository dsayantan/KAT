Problem-Solving

Puzzles, coding–decoding, directions, series, ranks, clocks, calendars, blood relations, syllogisms. 22 practice questions with worked answers. These are separate from the three online tests.

Question 1
Find the next letter: A, B, D, G, K, ?
  1. O
  2. Q
  3. P
  4. T
Show answer
Answer: C. P
Position gaps: 1, 2, 3, 4 – the gap rises by 1 each time, so the next gap is 5 and the letter is P.
Question 2
Starting from a point, Zoya walks 4 km towards north. She turns right and walks 5 km, then turns left and walks 8 km. How far is she from the starting point?
  1. 12 km
  2. 14 km
  3. 1 km
  4. 13 km
Show answer
Answer: D. 13 km
Draw it on a grid with north up: she ends at (5, 12) east/north of the start. Distance = √(5² + 12²) = 13 km.
Question 3
What is the smaller angle between the hands of a clock at 3:00?
  1. 180°
  2. 105°
  3. 150°
  4. 90°
Show answer
Answer: D. 90°
Angle = |30h − 5.5m| = |30×3 − 5.5×0| = 90°; the smaller angle is 90°. (The hour hand also moves as the minutes pass – ignoring that gives the trap value.)
