Online Test 3

30 multiple-choice questions plus one essay, 80 minutes, in the four-section order of the real KAT.

  • The clock starts when you press Begin and keeps running if you reload the page.
  • Change answers freely; nothing is marked until you submit. The test submits itself at time-up.
  • Your essay is saved in this browser as you type. It is not auto-marked; you self-assess it with a checklist.
  • No negative marking is applied (Krea has not published its scheme).
  • Suggested pacing: about 55 minutes for the 30 questions, 20 for the essay, 5 to review.
