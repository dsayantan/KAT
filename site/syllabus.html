KAT syllabus and pattern

What Krea has actually published, and what is our inference.

Published by Krea

ItemDetail
NameKrea Assessment Test (KAT), earlier called the Krea Aptitude Test
Duration80 minutes, taken online
Sections1. Problem-Solving · 2. Critical Thinking & Verbal Ability · 3. Numeric Reasoning · 4. Writing Skills
WritingReflective response, not more than 300 words
SampleSample test PDF (the 2022 and 2023–24 samples are identical) · Selection process

Krea publishes no detailed syllabus, no question count, no marking scheme and no past papers. The sample itself says it is “in no way representative of the length or number of questions”.

Topic map (our inference from the sample)

SectionTopics seen in the sampleLevel
Problem-SolvingLinear seating arrangement, letter coding (fixed and alternating shifts), blood relations, direction senseClass 8–10 reasoning
Critical Thinking & VerbalDrawing conclusions, spotting errors, rephrasing, vocabulary in contextClass 10–12 English
Numeric ReasoningPercentage, ratio, profit and cost, percentage differenceClass 8–10 arithmetic
WritingReflective essay on two linked ideasPersonal, 300 words

The practice sets also include nearby topics (series, ranks, clocks, calendars, syllogisms, interest, work and speed, assumptions, sentence order) because aptitude tests of this type commonly use them. Treat those as sensible preparation, not as announced content.

Source note

Those were not downloaded for this site, so the question level was calibrated from the Krea sample and general knowledge of such tests.
