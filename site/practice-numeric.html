Numeric Reasoning

Percentages, ratios, profit and loss, interest, speed, work, averages, mixtures, ages. 20 practice questions with worked answers. These are separate from the three online tests.

Question 1
Company A's revenue is 40% more than Company B's. By what percentage is B's revenue less than A's?
  1. 40%
  2. 66.67%
  3. 28.57%
  4. 20%
Show answer
Answer: C. 28.57%
Let B = 100, so A = 140. B is less than A by 40/140 × 100 = 28.57%. The base changes, so the percentages are not equal.
Question 2
In a town of 4000 people, 40% are children and 25% of the children are girls. How many girls are there?
  1. 1000
  2. 2600
  3. 400
  4. 1600
Show answer
Answer: C. 400
Children = 40% of 4000 = 1600. Girls = 25% of that = 400.
Question 3
The present ages of Rahul and Meera are in the ratio 3:8. After 10 years the sum of their ages will be 86. What is Rahul's present age?
  1. 21 years
  2. 48 years
  3. 18 years
  4. 28 years
Show answer
Answer: C. 18 years
Sum now = 86 − 2 × 10 = 66. One part = 66/11 = 6. Rahul = 3 × 6 = 18 years.
Question 4
The average of 4 numbers is 40. When one more number is included the average becomes 48. What is the new number?
  1. 80
  2. 88
  3. 40
  4. 48
Show answer
Answer: A. 80
Old total = 4 × 40 = 160. New total = 5 × 48 = 240. New number = 240 − 160 = 80.
Question 5

  1. 800
  2. 2800
  3. 2000
  4. 400
Show answer
Answer: D. 400
Children = 20% of 4000 = 800. Girls = 50% of that = 400.
Question 6
A car goes from P to Q at 40 km/h and returns along the same road at 60 km/h. What is its average speed for the whole trip?
  1. 24 km/h
  2. 50 km/h
  3. 48 km/h
  4. 53 km/h
Show answer
Answer: C. 48 km/h
For equal distances, average speed = 2ab/(a+b) = 2 × 40 × 60/100 = 48 km/h. The simple mean 50 is wrong because more time is spent at the slower speed.
Question 7
A can finish a job in 10 days and B in 15 days. Working together, in how many days will they finish it?
  1. 5 days
  2. 12.5 days
  3. 6 days
  4. 25 days
Show answer
Answer: C. 6 days
Work per day together: 1/10 + 1/15 = 25/150. Time = 150/25 = 6 days.
Question 8
A train running at 90 km/h passes a pole in 10 seconds. What is its length?
  1. 340 m
  2. 900 m
  3. 275 m
  4. 250 m
Show answer
Answer: D. 250 m
Speed = 90 × 5/18 = 25 m/s. Length = speed × time = 25 × 10 = 250 m.
Question 9
The price of a book rises from ₹120 to ₹144. By what percentage has the price increased?
  1. 40%
  2. 16.67%
  3. 20%
  4. 25%
Show answer
Answer: C. 20%
Increase = 144 − 120 = 24. % increase = 24/120 × 100 = 20%. Always divide by the original value.
Question 10
₹900 is divided among A, B and C in the ratio 4:5:6. How much does B receive?
  1. ₹360
  2. ₹240
  3. ₹300
  4. ₹500
Show answer
Answer: C. ₹300
Total parts = 15; one part = 900/15 = 60. B = 5 × 60 = ₹300.
Question 11
Find the simple interest on ₹2000 at 5% per annum for 5 years.
  1. ₹100
  2. ₹500
  3. ₹2500
  4. ₹1000
Show answer
Answer: B. ₹500
SI = P × R × T/100 = 2000 × 5 × 5/100 = ₹500.
Question 12
A shopkeeper marks goods 60% above cost and then allows a discount of 25%. What is his profit or loss percent?
  1. 15%
  2. 23%
  3. 20%
  4. 35%
Show answer
Answer: C. 20%
Take CP = 100. Marked = 160. SP = 160 × 0.75 = 120. Profit = 20%. (Not m − d = 35.)

Question 17
Two cars start at the same time from towns that are 300 km apart and drive towards each other at 40 km/h and 60 km/h. After how many hours do they meet?
  1. 15 hours
  2. 5 hours
  3. 7.5 hours
  4. 3 hours
Show answer
Answer: D. 3 hours
They close the gap at 40 + 60 = 100 km/h, so time = 300/100 = 3 hours.

  4. 60%
Show answer
Answer: A. 30%
Increase = 260 − 200 = 60. % increase = 60/200 × 100 = 30%. Always divide by the original value.
Question 19
Find the compound interest on ₹5000 for 2 years at 20% per annum, compounded yearly.
  1. ₹2200
  2. ₹7200
  3. ₹2000
  4. ₹3200
Show answer
Answer: A. ₹2200
Amount = 5000 × (1 + 20/100)² = 7200. CI = Amount − P = ₹2200. (Simple interest would be 2000; the extra 200 is interest on interest.)
Question 20

  4. ₹450
Show answer
Answer: D. ₹450
Total parts = 10; one part = 1500/10 = 150. B = 3 × 150 = ₹450.
